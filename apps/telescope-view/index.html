At 1× the eyepiece’s field fills the view. Raise it to inspect detail your eye would see at that size.

Every frame is computed from wave optics: the light through your aperture, obstruction and spider is Fourier-transformed at seven wavelengths into a diffraction pattern, bent by a drifting turbulent air layer and the refractor’s focus error at each colour, then blurred into the target. The planets are drawn procedurally, so their markings are plausible, not a map of tonight.
