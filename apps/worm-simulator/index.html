How the worm thinks

Food and water each drop by 1 every 2 seconds. If either hits zero, the worm dies. It eats until food is above 80, drinks until water is above 80, then crawls home to sleep. It wakes when food or water falls below 50.

Crawling costs energy and sleeping restores it. A worm that runs out of energy collapses where it is and sleeps until it has 40 back.

This is rule-based AI. A person wrote every rule above, and the worm never changes them, so the same food, water and energy always lead to the same choice. Nothing here learns. For a program that learns from examples, see Markov Pictures.

Sand grains push each other apart, and the worm's body shoves them aside, so it ploughs a path wherever it crawls.
