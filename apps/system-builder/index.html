            How it works
            • Everything is in real units and to scale. The map shows the system from above.
            • Stability checks run on every edit. Red means the system breaks; amber means it is at risk.
            • The dashed circle around the selected body is its Hill sphere, where its own gravity beats its parent's.
            • Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a body to open it, double-click to follow it. Esc closes a menu.
