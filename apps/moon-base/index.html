    Flight simulator

    Practise the trip home any time with a two-tank rocket. The base waits while you fly.

    Flying home

    The flight uses the real Earth and Moon: their masses, sizes and the 384,400 km between them, with the Moon going around the Earth every 27.3 days. Only the third dimension is gone.

    • AD turn, WS throttle, Z full, X cut.
    • P holds the nose along your motion, R against it.
    • ,. change time warp. While the engine burns, warp stops at 10×.
    • Mouse wheel or +− zoom.

    The blue line is where you will coast if you stop burning. Near the Moon it is drawn around the Moon; farther out, around the Earth.

    A route that works: burn straight up for a few seconds, tip over toward the horizon, and keep burning sideways until the far side of your path stays above the ground: you are in orbit at about 1.7 km/s. Then, on the side of the Moon facing away from its motion around the Earth, burn prograde until the line leaves the Moon and falls toward the Earth. Warp ahead and nudge the burn until the path ends in a green cross.

    To survive, the capsule has to hit the top of the atmosphere, 120 km up, between 5° and 8° below horizontal. Shallower and it skips back into space; steeper and it burns up. That window is the real one Apollo flew.
