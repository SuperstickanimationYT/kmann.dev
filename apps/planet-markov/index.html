Markov Planets

This model paints one pixel at a time, left to right, top to bottom. For each pixel it looks up what colors came next after the same surroundings in the training planets, and rolls a die weighted by those counts. That is the whole model.

What the model sees

The generator shows each planet from above its pole, so distance from the center is latitude.

Upscaling

The 96 px planet sets the big shapes. Each stage then doubles it with a second counting model: every new pixel looks at the low-res pixel it came from, which corner of it it is, and the pixels to its left and above. Stages learn from 3 planets rendered at 768 px.
