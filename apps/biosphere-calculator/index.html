Biosphere Calculator

This scores what a planet's energy and physics allow, not what would actually evolve. Body plans are sorted by what they do (catch light, hold up weight, move through a medium) rather than by Earth anatomy, but the list is still written by people who only know Earth. Zones are averages over latitude bands, or day side, twilight and night side for tidally locked worlds. Size limits scale Earth's record holders by gravity and air density.
