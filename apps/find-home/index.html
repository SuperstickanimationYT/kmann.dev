You are somewhere outside the Milky Way. Find the Sun and click it. The mission clock never stops; exposures add their length and every wrong claim costs an hour.

Camera

Wide-field camera with a 36 mm lens, framed like your eyes. It stacks 10 s frames; each takes 1 s of real time and adds 10 s to the clock. Your ship holds still until the exposure ends or you stop it.

Stars are procedural, except about 7,800 naked-eye stars within 3,000 light-years from Hipparcos. Nebulae: the 50 most ionizing star-forming complexes in Russeil (2003) plus Orion, Eagle, Lagoon, Rosette, North America and California, with rough distances and brightness. Spiral arms follow Reid et al. (2019). Everything you see is where it was when its light left, and the galaxy turns while that light travels.
