Every cell starts as the same simple alga: it photosynthesises and takes up nutrients. Each child gets slightly mutated genes, including how fast it mutates. Genes cost energy to keep, so nothing is free. Light is strongest at the top and nutrients sink, rising again only at two upwellings. Dead cells and scraps return to the water, so the total amount of matter never changes. There is no score: whatever survives and divides is what you see.
