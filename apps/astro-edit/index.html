Stretch
Enhance

This is a finishing tool, not a stacker: open an image that is already stacked or a single long exposure. It fits a smooth surface to the empty sky and divides and subtracts it away, which removes light pollution, moonlight gradients and lens vignetting. Then it brightens the faint end with an arcsinh stretch that keeps star colours, lifts structure in areas brighter than the sky, and smooths colour noise. Raw and TIFF files keep their full 16-bit linear data, so far more faint detail survives than with an edited JPEG.
