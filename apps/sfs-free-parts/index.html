Each saved rocket has a folder in the game's Saving/Blueprints folder holding its Blueprint.txt.

Load a rocket to swap its DLC parts for free ones.

To use it, copy any blueprint folder in Saving/Blueprints, give the copy a new name and replace the Blueprint.txt inside it.

    Δv is the vacuum figure from the SFS Δv Calculator. Parts the calculator has no mass for count as 0 t on both sides.

    Each DLC engine becomes a stack of free engines on the same spot, with the free engine whose Isp and mass come closest. Stretching an engine taller in a blueprint raises its thrust by the same factor without changing its Isp, so one engine in each stack is stretched or shrunk to match the thrust. No free engine comes near the ion engine's Isp, so ion rockets lose most of their Δv. DLC tanks become free tanks of the same size. Parts with no free stand-in yet are removed, because the game deletes DLC parts anyway when you don't own the DLC. The result can look a little odd, but it flies like the original.
