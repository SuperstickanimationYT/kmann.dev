How to read it
  • Everything is to scale: sizes and distances use the same ruler, shown bottom left.
  • A body smaller than a few pixels still gets a small dot so you can find it. Zoom in until it grows past the dot to see its true size.
  • Orbits are flattened onto one plane, seen from above.
  • A name ending in ? is an unconfirmed candidate. Sizes marked ≈ are estimates.
  • Green rings are conservative habitable zones, where liquid water could exist on a rocky surface.

Controls

  • Drag: pan. Wheel or pinch: zoom. Click: select. Double-click: fly to and follow.
  • Keys: Space pause, F whole system, +/- zoom, Esc deselect.
