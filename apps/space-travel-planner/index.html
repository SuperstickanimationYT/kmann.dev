      At the target

      Launcher capacity comes from NASA's Launch Services Program performance site. Engines are real: RL10, AJ10, R-4D and the Star 48BV solid motor. Cryogenic propellant boils off during the cruise, faster near the Sun, and hydrogen goes faster than oxygen. Once one runs short, the rest of the other is dead weight.

      Planet positions come from JPL's published orbital elements, and each trajectory is a two-body Lambert solution around the Sun. Mission planners start from the same model before they refine it with full gravity simulations. The brightest spot is the cheapest launch; the gold ring marks it.
