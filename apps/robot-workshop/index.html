Motors

    No motors yet. Pick the Motor tool, tap the beam that should turn, then tap a beam joined to it at the same joint. The motor turns the first beam against the second.

    Start from

    Loading replaces the robot on the board. Undo brings it back.

    Building

    Beams are rigid and pass through each other. Only joints touch the ground. Where two beams meet they pivot freely unless a motor drives them. Layers only organise the build: pick a layer number above the board to fade the rest, and new beams go on the chosen layer. A ringed joint is shared with another layer. Drag with the right mouse button or two fingers to pan, and scroll or pinch to zoom.
