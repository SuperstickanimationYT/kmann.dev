        Check yourself
        
        
Guess where a platform is, then check: the offset from your feet to its top, in your own directions.

        

      


      

        How to read the picture
        
The wireframe box is your camera's photo. Its three directions are right, up and ana. How far ahead something is shows as size: farther things are smaller and closer to the box's center.

        
Each platform looks like a flat box inside a flat box. The big outer one is its near side, the small inner one its far side.

        
Your white body stays at the same spot in the box. Watch the world move, not you.

        
To jump to a platform, turn until it sits directly above or below your body in the box. Then it is straight ahead, and only the distance is left to judge.

        
    
          
  • WS zoom the scene in or out.
    • 
          
  • AD slide it left or right; QE slide it front to back.
    • 
          
  • ZX turn it like a turntable without changing sizes. Learn this turn first.
    • 
          
  • The drop line under you ends in a cross where you would land. It turns red over the void.
    • 
          
  • Each platform casts a shadow straight down onto the box's floor, joined to it by a dashed line. Read the shadow to see where the platform sits left to right and front to back inside the box.
    • 
          
  • Brighter, thicker lines are nearer to you inside the box, not in the 4D world.
    • 
          
  • If the box seems to flip inside out, turn on the wobble.
    • 
        

        
The levels teach one move at a time, starting in 3D. In 3D, the outside view shows where your camera points.
